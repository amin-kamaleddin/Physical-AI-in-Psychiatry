# Author actions required before submission or public release

1. Supply the final author list, order, degrees, affiliations, ORCID identifiers, corresponding-author email, telephone, and postal affiliation.
2. Approve a CRediT contribution statement naming every author by initials.
3. Supply the funding/acknowledgement statement and all financial and non-financial competing interests.
4. Select an OSI-approved license and replace the copyright-holder placeholder in `LICENSE`.
5. Deposit a tagged release in a DOI-minting repository; update `CITATION.cff`, Data Availability, and Code Availability.
6. Review and approve the draft ChatGPT-use disclosure in the Methods.
7. Decide whether to obtain empirical parameter elicitation, stakeholder face validation, or external service-data validation before targeting npj Digital Medicine.
8. Decide whether the operational criterion should remain zero-margin non-worsening or use clinically meaningful prespecified margins.
9. Complete the Nature Portfolio Reporting Summary and Editorial Policy Checklist.
10. Disclose related manuscripts, preprints, conference abstracts, or overlapping submissions in the cover letter and submission system.
